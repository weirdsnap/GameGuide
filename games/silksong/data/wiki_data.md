## Bell Beast

The Bell Beast is an NPC and boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. She was first seen in the Silksong reveal trailer. The Bell Beast appears to be the main method of fast travel throughout Pharloom. She is initially found in the Marrow, where she is trapped in Silk which can only be cleared by a Silkspear attack. Once the Bell Beast is freed, she attacks Hornet. After her defeat, she can be used to travel between the different bell stations. In Act 3 she has babies.

## Behavior and Tactics

The Bell Beast has three main attacks:

Charge: Bell Beast digs in the ground for a moment, without making any growling noises, before surfacing and rushing at Hornet.

Launch: Bell Beast digs in the ground, similar to the charge attack, but growls before jumping up in the air and arching to land on the other side of the arena.

Throw: Bell Beast silently digs in the ground, with a smaller width than with the other attacks, before launching a bell on both sides of herself. Each bell, when thrown with this attack, bounces once before rolling to either edge of the arena and rolls off the edges of the screen.

Bell Beast will always start the battle with a Charge attack starting from the right side of the arena.

After each attack, Bell Beast returns to momentarily hide underground. Once you're in the second battle phase, any attack has a chance to make one bell fall from the ceiling, bouncing once before passing through the ground.

To free her, you need the Silk Spear skill.

Unlike The Last Stag, she does not appear to have any dialogue.

Bell Beast carrying Hornet in the Silksong reveal trailer

Bell Beast does double damage with all attacks, including falling bells. She enters Phase 2 at 15 hits (Silk Spear does 3 hits) after a scream. In this phase, Bell Beast will cause a bell to fall from the roof after every attack, telegraphed by dust falling from the ceiling. These bells bounce once and then crumble. The fight ends at around 30 hits. PS: (one "hit" is 5 damage)

## Trivia

- The Bell Beast is visually very similar to the Dirtcarver.

- While there is no option to interact with the Bell Beast, hitting her with your needle allows you to "pet" the Bell Beast. She enjoys this by purring and playing with the ground.

- Using the Needolin ability near the Bell Beast, she starts to purr. However, there is no special dialogue upon doing so.

- After defeating the Bell Eater in Act 3, it's revealed that the Bell Beast has children, and can allow Hornet to access the Bell Beast via the Beastling Call ability w/ the Needolin.

- You can juggle the Beastlings with your needle. Very fun activity to do if you are bored.

## Citadel

The Citadel is a late-game area in Pharloom. It is located at the very top of the kingdom. Hornet must ascend to the Citadel on her quest through Pharloom. The Citadel has been often described as "shining" and "holy".

In order to access the Citadel, Hornet must unlock 5 bells and ring them across the map. Upon doing so, the entrance to the Citadel will be accessible, although before entering, Hornet is interrupted by the Last Judge. After defeating the Last Judge, the Great Citadel will open. If the entrance is reached before all the bells have been rung, an NPC will mark the locations on the map. additionally the citadel can be reached through The Mist.

## Clover Dancers

The Clover Dancers is a boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong.

The boss fight is in the memory of the Green Prince. You must use the Elegy of the Deep on him when he is in Verdania, which is only accessible in Act 3 and is located to the right of Craw Lake.

After you use the Elegy of the Deep, you enter Lost Verdania, the memory of what the Green Prince's land used to be. Once you collect 12 glowing orbs, you are able to enter the Green Prince's home and fight the Clover Dancers. The fight is a 2v1, with you fighting the Green Prince and the memory of his unnamed partner. It is very similar to the Cogwork Dancers, though you cannot damage the Green Prince's partner.

The Green Prince and his partner were the two rulers of Verdania. As the Citadel grew, the two princes were worried that they would try to take their territory. So, the Green Prince's partner sacrificed himself to prevent their land from being taken. However, he was made into the model bug for the Cogwork Dancers. Verdania died out, so the Green Prince was locked into Sinner's Road.

## Cogwork Dancers

The Cogwork Dancers are a duo boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. These bosses are found in the Cogwork Core of The Citadel. The Cogwork Dancers move in sync with each other, with white lines choreographing their movement. This boss has 4 phases.

## Behavior

Phase 1: Both dancers will move at the same time in the direction of their white line. Occasionally they will create a circle in the middle which creates a barrage of slashes. They can also create a bunch of lines across the arena and cut across them rapidly.

Phase 2: Both dancers are much faster.

Phase 3: The dancers now move one at a time, out of sync.

Phase 4: After 1 dancer dies, the other one will become extremely slow. They will attempt to do their circle barrage attack, but without their partner, they simply float slowly to the ground.

Note: Prior to the start of each phase, the cogs in the background will wind up the dancers accordingly. Before the first phase, the cogs wind up normally. Before the second, they wind up noticeably faster. Before the third, they take turns winding up one at a time. Before the final phase, the remaining dancer’s cogs wind up slowly.

- Order In The Court: Crawfather reels his head back, before pecking the ground three times, moving forward with each attack.

- Witness To The Stand: Crawfather screams, summoning either two Craw Jurors, one Tallcraw Juror, or one Squatcraw Juror.

- Justice From On High: Crawfather takes flight, flutters for a short while, then dashes towards Hornet with his beak.

### Phase 2

When Crawfather takes enough damage, he gains a new attack :

- Chains For The Penitent: Crawfather flutters at a medium height, sending chains in all directions. After a short while, the chains erupt in barbed spikes, dealing two damage on hit. After this, Crawfather falls back to the ground, striking with his beak down below.

## Fourth Chorus

Fourth Chorus is a boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. It appears in Deep Docks after completing the Flexile Spines Quest in Far Fields and receiving the Drifters Cloak cloak upgrade from The Seamstress. It's described as a metal automation that works in the Deep Docks.

In the first phase of the fight, it has two main attacks: it slams down vertically with one of its two top hands, or swipes horizontally across the screen with one of its bottom two hands. The vertical attack temporarily destroys the platform underneath it, so Hornet cannot stand on it. Falling into the lava there deals two masks of damage. The boss only takes damage from hits on its head, since the rest of its body is a sturdy metal.

Once Fourth Chorus has staggered, another attack is added, which causes lava balls to rain down from the ceiling. Coming into contact with one does one mask of damage.

In the final stage of the fight, which comes after 3 staggers, upward drafts will appear to the left and right of the battle platform, which is now slightly smaller. While it's possible to beat Fourth Chorus by attacking its head until it falls, it can also be defeated indirectly. This can be done when the upward drafts show up, by riding them to the top and hitting the magma blocks (one on each side) in the ceiling. This causes a Boulder to fall and crush the boss's head.

## Grand Mother Silk

Grand Mother Silk is one of the main bosses in Hollow Knight: Silksong. She is the main antagonist of Hollow Knight: Silksong.

## Lore

At first, Weavers were free from her for the first time, and some had even left Pharloom for other kingdoms, such as Hallownest. However, the Bugs of Pharloom, specifically those in the Whiteward, used the mother's Silk in order to increase their lifespan, which led to the Grand Mother being able to control these bugs at her will. She had hated what the Weavers had done to her, so she commanded the Bugs of Pharloom to venture out of their kingdom in order to find the Weavers, which had included Hornet despite also being part Wyrm.

Weavers had also made Weavenests across Pharloom . They were outposts in which Weavers tested out experiments. The most notable Weavenest is Weavenest Atla, where Hornet encounters Eva. A being made purely of Silk, in an attempt to recreate a Higher Being who could challenge the Grand Mother Silk. However, Eva's form was too frail to exist outside her chamber.

## In-game events

## Behaviour and tactics

Grand Mother Silk functions similarly to the Radiance, in that she does not deal contact damage and is also attacked at the head. Instead of teleporting like the Radiance, she swiftly moves around and launches sets of nails either vertically down or horizontally in the direction Hornet is in. She also has one of the few moves in the game that deals 3 masks of damage.

Grand Mother Silk is located at the top of The Cradle.

## Gurr the Outcast

|

Huge, yet stealthy Skarr. An expert trapper and master of the undergrowth.

His tracks and traps... It should have been obvious from the start, this old hunter sought to draw me in. But what reason drove him to test himself so brutally? That answer I suspect I shall never know.

Gurr the Outcast is an optional boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. He's located in a secret room in Far Fields

## In-game events

After starting act 3, a new wish will appear on Bellhart's wish wall : "The hidden hunter", which requests you to track down Gurr. New traps are added in the way to him and the last tracking object is in reality a cage which will bring you down to his arena. The fight will start shortly after.

## Behaviour and Tactics

- Trap Throw: Gurr leaps into the air, either vertically or diagonally, and throws 2 Barbed Traps in succession that float midair in random locations throughout the arena. After a few moments, traps extend their spikes into a large circle, then disappear. If Hornet hits a trap, it expands immediately. After reaching 750 HP, Gurr always throws out 3 traps instead. When this attack is finished, Gurr can either land back on the floor or immediately dive beneath the ground.

- Slide: Gurr hunches down and slides across the arena, only stopping when he reaches a wall. Upon reaching a wall, he can either perform another attack or immediately burrow down.

- Floor Spikes: When Gurr is above ground, he makes a small jump up and dive downwards into the ground. The ground then rumbles as 3 sets of spikes shoot up in random positions across the ground, then retract after a short time. The positions of the spikes are telegraphed by small puffs of dust.

- Trap Flurry: Once per fight at 400 HP, Gurr performs this attack; signalled by him shrieking while underground. After a moment, Gurr leaps out of the ground in the very centre of the arena and throws 5 Barbed Traps in succession around the room.

Leap: Gurr makes large leaps around the arena to reposition for attacks.
Backroll: If Hornet is too close to Gurr after an attack, he quickly rolls away a set distance.

## Needolin dialogue

Gurr the Outcast does not respond to the Needolin during battle. The following dialogue can be found in the room to the left of the arena :

- ...The last...

- ...The hunt...

- ...Pale...prey...

- ...Come to me...

## Location

Gurr can be found in Far Fields after promising the wish "The hidden hunter".

## Hornet (Silksong)

For the Hollow Knight character and boss, see Hornet.

|

Hornet, princess-protector of Hallownest, finds herself alone in a vast, unfamiliar world.

– Official website

Hornet is the mysterious princess-protector of Hallownest’s ruins, the deuteragonist of Hollow Knight and the main protagonist of Hollow Knight: Silksong. She wields a Needle and thread with deadly prowess.

Hornet is captured by outsider bugs and brought to their kingdom, Pharloom, but she fortunately escapes her cage before arriving at their destination.

## Health and Silk

### Health

Health is represented as Masks, shown in the upper-left corner of the screen. Hornet starts the game with 5 Masks and can obtain more. Health is lost by taking damage, either from enemies or from environmental hazards. Health can be regained by the use of the Bind ability consuming a full Silk spool.

Lifeblood Masks, granted by Lifeblood needles or Lifeblood cocoon, are temporary masks.

### Silk

Silk is represented as a spool below the health indicator. Thread is consumed when using special abilities, including a form of Focus named Bind. Striking enemies with the Needle fills the spool by one lace, up to a total of nine. Other effects (such as from tools) may fill laces partially, with different style of filling up lace. When the spool is full, the symbol representing the side of the spool left to the health indicator is filled, indicating a Bind is possible.

##
Weapon and Tools

Needle

The Needle is Hornet's main weapon. Not only can she use it to slash or block attacks against enemies, but also to dash forward, stab, and bounce up above them. She can also use it alongside her abilities to deal greater damage or as a grappling hook.

Crests

Crests are items that act effectively like a character class template. Each one alters Hornet's move set, attack patterns, Bind skill and the allowed spread of Tools that Hornet can equip; Hornet starts with the Hunter Crest. Crests can be changed when sitting on a Bench similar to Charms.

Tools

Tools are special items classed by categories, equipped on a Crest with limited slots. They can be used as throwing weapons or defensive aids without costing Silk. Tools can be changed when sitting on a Bench similar to Charms.

## Abilities

Abilities mentioned don't consume Silk unless specified so.

### Swift Step

Swift Step is a Fabled Weaver talent.

This talent lets you dash forward and sprint with unnatural speed, as though gliding upon silk.

You can perform a dive dash to land quicker.

### Cling Grip

Cling Grip is an Ancient Weaver technique.

This talent allows you to cling to sheer surfaces and leap off to rapidly scale heights.

Pressing dash prompt allows you to climb a short distance and then jump if you reach edge.

### Needolin

Needolin is a Holy Weaver talent.

Needolin allows you to Play ancient melodies by stringing Silk along the needle.

Needolin uses Silk thread at a speed of 10 seconds a lace.

The Needolin is used in various places across Pharloom to uncover main content as well as secrets.

### Clawline

Clawline is an Advanced Weaver talent. It can be obtained in the Underworks at The Cauldron. The White key is required to enter this area and acquire the talent.

This talent costs Silk to throw the Silk-strung needle like a harpoon and charge toward it. It cannot be used if the character has no silk on the Spool.

If Hornet strikes a enemy with Clawline, she will
charge, hit the enemy and refund the Silk used.

### Silk Soar

Ancient Weaver talent. Throw the needle upward and launch to tremendous heights.

This ability consumes one Silk and channel Hornet to shoot the needle upward, if it hit a ceiling, Hornet will follow and ascend.

This ability can be found in the Abyss at Weavenest Absolom.

### Sylphsong

Essense of a being merged within. Silk will be restored when resting at a bench.

This ability can be found at Moss Grotto in Weavenest Atla, by completing Eva's request.

## Lace

|

A lithe fencer wielding a gilded pin, the traditional weapon of Pharloom. Lace is a dangerous foe, cloaked in mystery, who’ll delight at causing Hornet’s swift, skewered demise.

– Official description

Lace is a recurring boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong.

Lace makes two appearances in two different arenas in the reveal trailer as an antagonist.

In the first trailer appearance Lace and Hornet battle in a dark arena on a bed of white roses with massive pipes trailing in the background. A big golden cage is also present in the centre of the arena. Lace does a swipe up with her "Gilded Pin" over Hornet's head before propelling herself down at an angle with a midair jab.

The second trailer appearance is very brief as for a second Lace is shown to be wildly swiping and dashing around mid-air as if in some sort of frenzy. The new setting is also on a metal platform over a pool of lava.

The second appearance over the lava lake was revealed to be Hornet's first encounter with Lace during the game.

## Behaviour and Tactics

In her first encounter and fight in the Deep Docks Lace uses the following attacks and manoeuvres:

- Lunge: Lace dashes forwards with her pin extended across the platform, covering roughly 50% of it.

- Double Cut: Lace slashes twice, making large arcs over her head with her pin. Each slash moves her forwards a slight distance.

- Balestra: Lace launches herself at an angle into the air and slashes a full circle around her body with her pin once she reaches the peak of the jump. She then dashes at an angle towards the ground with a jab, skidding a short bit after landing. While at the peak of her jump, Lace can change the direction of her jab depending on Hornet's position.

- Parry & Riposte: Lace holds her pin up, striking a defensive position and blocking any damage. She holds this position for about half a second, and if she is struck in that time, she retaliates with a small dash forwards chained into a quick flurry of strikes with her pin. If Hornet is caught in her flurry, she initially takes one Mask of damage and then is held in place by the blows until Lace slashes again with her pin; dealing an additional Mask of damage. If Hornet is not caught in this attack, Lace's flurry of strikes does not last as long.

- Flourish: During the end of an attack, Lace adds a small flair. Sometimes she fixes and straightens her stance with a small slash from her pin. Doing this deals damage to Hornet if she is caught in it, and deflects projectiles that are thrown at her. Other times Lace simply strikes a quick harmless bow or pose before continuing the fight.

- Retreat: Lace quickly glides back a short distance to reposition for another attack.

- Advance: Lace takes a short half-step forwards, accompanied by a very small flick of her Pin. She sometimes chains this move to traverse the arena and reposition.

- Whirlwind: Lace stands in place for a brief moment charging up, in this time a white circle is highlighted mid-air somewhere in the arena. After charging, Lace unleashes a flurry of 4 slashes within the highlighted region. At the end of her last slash, Lace slams down from that position, shaking the platform. This attack is only used when Lace reaches phase 2(marked by Lace staggering). If Hornet is caught in this attack Lace deals one Mask of damage, traps Hornet within the Whirlwind and then slams Hornet down onto the platform, dealing an additional Mask of damage.

## Last Judge

Last Judge is a boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong found in the Blasted Steps after reaching the Citadel Gate, and completing the Grand Gate quest, which requires finding and activating all five grand bells throughout Pharloom.

## Behavior

Last Judge has 3 phases and 720 health.

### Phase 1:

- Spin - Last Judge spins her bell in a circle before shortly spinning it around herself for ~2 seconds.

- Toss - Last Judge tosses her bell forward in the air, before reeling it back in across the ground.

- Slam - Last Judge will jump above Hornet and slam her bell into the ground.

- Jump - Last Judge will jump over Hornet.

### Phase 2: +Fire

- Ignite - Upon entering phase 2, Last Judge will ignite her bell with fire.

- Fire Spin - During the flail attack, choreographed rings of sparks will spawn throughout the arena before activating and lighting on fire.

- Fire Toss - After Last Judge throws her bell, it will shortly explode within a small radius before she reels it back in.

- Fire Slam - After Last Judge slams her bell onto the ground, the floor of the entire arena will shortly catch on fire.

- Fire Dash - After a short windup, Last Judge will charge toward Hornet, spawning pillars of fire around the arena.

### Phase 3: +Speed

- Long Spin - Upon entering phase 3, Last Judge will always perform an extended version of Fire Flail which lasts roughly 8 seconds before shortly stumbling to her knees.

- Fire Spin, Fire Toss, Fire Slam, and Fire Dash - Exact same as phase 2, but much much faster.

### Phase 4: Death

- Dying Wish - Upon dying smoke will emanate from the Last Judge's armour, and begin to release a gigantic cloud of sparks. After a few seconds these sparks will ignite and explode roughly 10 times. If you are caught in the blast and die, you will have to fight Last Judge again.

## Lore

- The Last Judge is a long-lived bug tasked with letting only the worthy into the Citadel.

- The Last Judge's flail is a censer, which is a vessel used to burn incense and usually made out of metals like bronze or gold.

## Moorwing

### Location

Moorwing is an enemy and boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong, and can be found in the Greymoor area left of the Bellway station.

### Attacks and Behaviour

The Moorwing has 3 different attacks in phase 1, those being a side dive, many quick slashes downwards and throwing a bone saw along the ground. In phase 2 it gains a few new moves including throwing two bone saws in a circle downwards making you have to jump twice and the phase 1 bone saw move, but with two saws instead of just one. The Moorwing will also fall, so make sure you're not beneath it, or you will be dealt 2 masks of damage.

### Appearance

Moorwing has brownish wings and two teeth, and kind of looks like a big fly.

### Strategy

During the first phase, you can stay under it and attack upwards to send it up. It is also best to try to keep it in the middle of the battlefield so if you die, you can get your soul back before restarting the battle, and too far right will put objects in the way of combat. The Moorwing attacks will start slightly in a horizontal direction, allowing you to dash out of the way. If you have the drifter's cloak, you can float above the saws instead of jumping, making it much easier to avoid the double saws in phase 2. Remaining below the Moorwing while the circular saws are being thrown will also allow you to only jump once to avoid them. The best time to heal is generally while the Moorwing is making a multi-slash, or immediately after you avoid the side dive. Alternatively, you can pogo on top of the boss, greatly negating the difficulty of dodging all of its attacks

### Trivia

- You can fight Moorwing with Garmond and Zaza by requesting them to aid you in battle. You will have to request their aid every time you leave the game.

- Hornet is shown fighting Moorwing alongside Garmond and Zaza in the Xbox trailer.

- In the Hunter's Journal, Moorwing is described as being a mite-like creature.

- Moorwing's fight can be skipped if the Flea Caravan is moved there before Moorwing is killed.

- If Moorwing is fought in Act 3, a harder, Void-infused version of it can be fought.

## Moss Mother

Moss Mother is a boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong, being the first boss fought by Hornet. When entering her arena the room starts to rumble, then she explodes out of a plant-like cocoon in the middle of the arena and lets out a screech signalling the start of the fight.

- Swoop: Moss Mother flies down in a small dive with her stinger pointed in Hornet's direction. When she reaches the floor she slides her body across roughly half of the arena. For this attack, it helps to down slash on her midway through the swoop.

## Quests

Quests (or tasks) are a system in Hollow Knight: Silksong that Hornet may accept from NPCs found across Pharloom. Ongoing Quests appear on notice boards that may be found in various towns.

These Quests are divided into categories:

- Donate: Pay a toll, usually shell shards or Rosaries.

- Gather: Find and collect materials.

- Wayfarer: Related to locating or Helping a lost NPC.

- Learn: Gather information about the world.

- Hunt: Killing a specific enemy or group of enemies or some other form of combat trial.

- Grand Hunt: killing a boss or a mini-boss .

- Sprint: Win a race against Sprintmaster Swift

- Witness: Find Mister Mushroom in various locations across Pharloom

## Silk

Silk is a unique force that acts as the life-force for bugs in Pharloom. Similar to its counterpart, Soul, it can be manipulated into spells and healing, but it can also be used to power up machines and automatons.

In-game, Silk is represented on the HUD by a spool with several strands. Hornet starts with a broken spool, only able to hold 9 strands of Silk. When the maximum amount of strands are reached, they glow a white light. Silk accumulated in the extended Spool is smaller and more dim, but function the exact same. When Hornet dies, her Spool becomes broken and can only hold 9 Strands of Silk.

## Lore

Silk is a beneficial force that can extend life and heal wounds that is found in almost every bug in Pharloom. Strands of Silk can be seen in places such as the Mossy Grotto, and when unlocking Silk hearts, they form around in the air before combining into the Heart. Bugs of Pharloom, especially those in the Whiteward, stored Silk in spindles, and have also recorded certain surgeries for injecting Silk into bugs.

Silk can be used to channel Silk Skills, such as Silkspear, Thread Storm, Sharpdart and Rune Rage which were made by the Weavers. Other skills such as Cross Stitch come from a special technique from Phantom, and Pale Nails which is a move created by the Grand Mother Silk herself. Many tools interact with Silk as well. Injector Bands increase the speed of binding and Spool Extender allows one to hold more Silk.

The bugs in Whiteward used Silk as a means to extend the life of their surrounding bugs, but generations of surgery lead to the Grand Mother to gain control over them, even while asleep. Many bugs and clans attempted to resist the Haunting, with notable examples being the Green Princes of Verdania and the Skarr Clan. However, they have weakened and most of their inhabitants either died or fell victim to the Haunting.

Hornet uses her Needle to gain Silk by striking enemies, as well as using spindles across the Citadel and Webs found in places such as Mount Fay.

## Usage

Silk can be used to cast Silk Skills or to Bind, which heals Hornet 3 masks in a single instance. When there enough Silk to cast a Silk Skill, if equipped, the icon of the Silk Skill glows white. If there is enough Silk to Bind, then the Silk Spool's threads glow White accompanied by a soft sound effect.

Binding requires 9 Silk and only heals 3 masks. This can be increased to 4 when using the Multibinder tool. Casting Silk Skills cost 4 Silk each, but can be reduced to 3 using the Egg of Flealia tool.

Some Ancestral Arts also require Silk. Each Art requires 1 Silk each, with the exception of Needolin since it uses Silk over time. Pressing Battle Stance also expends Silk unless you have the Throwing Rings equipped.

The Silkshot tool is the only tool that requires Silk to be used, using 1 Silk per attack.

## Acquiring Silk

### Attacking Enemies

Enemies struck with the Needle cause Hornet to gain Silk at a rate of 1 strand per hit.

### Silk Spindles

Silk Spindles are found across all of Pharloom. When hit with the Needle, they release Strands of Silk that are absorbed, granting 3 Strand of Silk each time they are struck. After a certain number of hits, the Silk Spindle runs out and no longer gives Silk. They are similar to Hollow Knight's Soul Totems.

### Webs

Webs are found all across Pharloom. There are webs on sets of vines, potentially left by Weavers. Each web gives 3 silk strands.

### Tools

Certain tools may help with the acquiring of Silk, like Druid's Eye and Weavelight. Some tools increase the Spool length like Spool Extender.

### Sylphsong

Essence of a being merged within. Silk will be restored when resting at a bench.

Requires using 18 Memory Lockets on any crest besides the Hunter's crest and obtaining all previous rewards from Eva. Works exactly the same as Salubra's Blessing in Hollow Knight.

### Hot Springs

Only 2 Hot Springs exist in Silksong: one in the Citadel Spa and the other in the Flea Caravan. Generates Silk at the same rate as Sylphsong.

## Silk Spool

Silk Spools are additional storage for Silk, represented as an extension to the original Spool. Hornet can collect 9 Silk Spools throughout the game by collecting Spool Fragments. 2 Spool Fragments are required to make one Silk Spool. Each Spool holds 1 strand of Silk.

If Hornet gains silk if the original Spool is full, it is instantly transferred to the next empty spool, starting with the closest leftmost one.

Unlike Hollow Knight, Silk does not need to be transferred from Spools to the Main Spool, and there is no delay for transferring Silk between Spools. Also, Hornet does not gain Silk at a reduced rate for the extended Spools.

## Trivia

- Silkspear, Thread Storm and Sharpdart are all moves used by Hornet in her boss fight in Hollow Knight. However, Silkspear and Sharpdart are quicker with different animations from the boss fight. Thread Storm remains the exact same, with the exception of spamming the attack move increases the duration.

- Battle Stance has the exact same animation as the one used in the boss fight.

- Beast's Crest changes the animation of Battle Stance to a more upright stance with her needle facing down and opposite of where Hornet is facing. Hornet's voice also becomes more raspy.

- Using Throwing Rings with Battle Stance does not expend silk and is identical to Shakra's idle after walking away from her.

## The Mists

The Mist is a secret area accessed through Bilewater and a breakable wall in Sinners road. A treacherous illusion conjured by Phantom, the Mist serves to hide the Exhaust Organ used to expel the choking refuse of the Citadel.

## Description

The entrance of the Mist is a pathway with several dead moorbugs like thread rakers, roach feeders and roach catchers scattered about

Apart from small groups of Silkflies the only lifeforms, if one could call them that, populating the area are Wraiths that hover motionlessly in the air until Hornet comes too close, at which point they will scream and lunge at her, draining her of life and silk upon making contact.

Blind movement will get her nowhere, but not all rooms are dangerous. Some smaller ones contain only Silkflies. If the Needolin is played near the Silkflies, they will guide Hornet towards the correct exit until she finally reaches the Exhaust Organ. If Hornet goes through the wrong exit or Falls into the mist-covered pits it will put Hornet back at the entrance to the mist.

Defeating Phantom will give you the Cross Stitch spell and the Mist will become inaccessible putting you straight infront of the exhaust organ. The exhaust organ will remain as a sub-area of Bilewater.

## How to Access

The Mist is located at the bottom of Bilewater, on the opposite side of the area's Bellway station. They additionally can be reached by breaking a wall on the left side of the left room after ascending the main room connected to the first bench in sinner’s road, connecting to the aforementioned entrance.

## The Unraveled

The Unravelled is an optional Act 2 boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. It drops a Silk Heart upon defeat.

The Unravelled is located in the Whitewardsection of the game, in order to fight it you will need to have unlocked the Ancestral Art; Clawline and acquired the Surgeon's Key.

Start from the Bench located in Whiteward, then proceed towards the west side of Whiteward to look for the Ornate Lock for which you may use the Surgeon's Key.

After the lock has been unlocked you may proceed all the way down until you reach the location of The Unravelled.

## Tormented Trobbio

|

Depressed thespian, consumed by the melancholy of a collapsing world.

This one chose to embrace the doom to come. I shall not share such easy surrender.

Tormented Trobbio is an optional boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. He's located in The Stage in the Whispering Vaults.

## In-game events

After starting act 3, the player must accept the "Pain, Anguish and Misery" wish, which invites them to return to The Stage, where Trobbio has previously been fought.

## Behaviour and Tactics

### Phase 1

- Fly: Tormented Trobbio uses his wings to fly across the arena.

- Floorboard Shuffle: Tormented Trobbio descends into the floorboards to traverse to the opposite end of the arena.

- Woeful Whirlwind: Tormented Trobbio spins, creating a tornado that goes horizontally while leaving trails of explosion in its path. If the attack is used while Trobbio is in midair, the tornado slowly descends while still leacing the explosive trail.

- Forlorn Fireworks: Tormented Trobbio creates an plus or cross shaped explosion around him, and multiple expllosions happen across the arena shortly after.

- Smoke Bombs: Tormented Trobbio throws 3 smoke bombs that explodes either in a plus or cross shape. He can throw them either while being underground or on the arena.

### Phase 2

When Tormented Trobbio takes enough damage, he gains a new attack :

- Pyrotechnics: Trobbio disappears underground, before 5 fire pillars shoot out from the ground from random locations and at staggered intervals. The pillars are telegraphed by a fizzle coming from under the floor where they appear. Trobbio is launched into the air from within the fifth fire pillar, then 2 more pillars shoots out.

## Needolin Dialogue

Despite not being haunted, Tormented Trobbio willingly pauses mid-battle to sing as the Needolin is played.

- Woe...Woe...

- Fell fated fallen!

- Pain... Despair... Torment...

- Alone against the dark...

- Scorned to suffer...

- Light's final gleam...

## Location

Tormented Trobbio can be found in The Stage, in the Whispering Vaults after promising the "Pain, Anguish and Misery" wish.

## Trobbio

|

Master of the Stage, the Brush and the Voice, the flamboyant butterfly Trobbio seeks fame and adoration throughout Pharloom.

– Official description

Trobbio is an NPC and boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong, first revealed in the game's announcement trailer.

Trobbio's first appearance was in the announcement trailer; he is shown in a dark room with several light fixtures visible in the background, alongside a large bell symbol. He stands opposite Hornet, before hunching over as several plumes of steam erupt from the floor. Before long, he raises up and loudly shouts his name, expanding his wings with a scarlet flash as confetti erupts from most of the areas the plumes of steam previously erupted from.

His name was later directly revealed in a blog post, which described him as the "Master of the Stage, the Brush and the Voice", and states he is seeking fame and adoration throughout Pharloom.

In-game, Trobbio is found in The Stage as a boss encounter, a small area which is first accessible through the Whispering Vaults. It is recommended that when going up against him, you first set your respawn point in the Grand Bellway, as it provides an easy way to get back there for in case you die.

Trobbio has 4 phases in his fight. After the second, his attacks become more rapid and he gains the ability to summon fireworks within the ground.

Upon defeating him, Trobbio drops the Claw Mirror.

He can be fought again in Act 3 after accepting a wish, becoming purple and having harder attacks. After being defeated, he descends via an elevator dramatically and drops an upgrade to the Claw Mirror. If you go further away and come back again, he peeks out of the floor, spots Hornet, and goes back down.

The player can also take his feather and use it to update your map, but when he turns purple you can get the purple feather

- Although he was revealed under the "New Bosses" title card in the announcement trailer, in the Silksong press kit, his art is labelled under the NPCs section.

## Voltvyrm

|

Coiled nest of charged worms. Assaults intruders with bolts of crackling electricity.

Barely a bug, and possessing only basic thought, yet instinct alone proved enough to turn it deadly.

Voltvyrm is an optional boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong. It's located in the Voltnest

## In-game events

By exploring the Sands of Karak, the player can enter a sub-area called the Voltnest. Eventually, they can encounter Voltvyrm in a room in the bottom left of the zone. Stepping into the room will cause the fight to start. When defeated, the player can claim the Volt filament on its corpse.

## Behaviour and Tactics

- Electric Spark: Voltvyrm will create pink circles that will expand and unleash electricity after a short time

- Electric Pillars: Pale pink pillars with glowing circles will appear. Electricity will then appear on the pillars after a short time.

## Needolin dialogue

Voltvyrm does not respond to the Needolin.

## Location

Voltvyrm can be found in Voltnest, at the top of the vertical central room.

## Widow

Widow is a boss in Hollow Knight: Silksong located in Bellhart. She is the responsible of the curse casted upon Bellhart that covers the whole zone with silk threads.

"Crazed fanatic able to guide Pharloom's haunted Silk by music played upon it. Her body seemed a Weaver's, but her mask had been forcibly removed, and her own Silk was bound useless by heavy pins lodged through her spine." Entry from the Journal

Phase 1:

- Launches bells with either a straight or diagonal trajectory.

- Appears in the air then slashes downwards diagonally and slashes towards you dealing two masks of damage.

- Runs across the floor and after 3 seconds summons bells from the ground dealing two masks of damage.

- Summons bells in large area of screen dealing two masks of damage.

Phase 2:

once you beat her for the first time she comes back faster

- She Stops summoning Bells

- Her slash attacks starts on the floor

- The diagonal bells are summoned in groups of 2

- downward bells summoned in three's

Strategy:

- Phase 1:

- Don't worry about the falling bells they only deal one mask of damage and are easy to predict

- When she scuttles in the air she is going to do her slash attack so wall climb or pogo when she lands
